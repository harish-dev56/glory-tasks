About

This is a mobile reward application where users can earn coins by completing tasks such as watching ads, spinning a reward wheel, and participating in surveys.

Features

  • Watch Ads & Earn Coins
  • Spin Wheel Rewards
  • Daily Bonus
  • Survey Tasks
  • Offerwall Tasks (Apps & Engagement)

How It Works

Users complete tasks to earn coins. Coins can be collected and redeemed according to the app reward system.
